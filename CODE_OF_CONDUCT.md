# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We welcome contributors from every background, identity, and experience level.

We commit to an open, respectful, inclusive, and harassment-free community.

## Expected behavior

- Show empathy and kindness.
- Respect different viewpoints and experiences.
- Give constructive feedback.
- Accept responsibility and correct mistakes.
- Focus on the community's long-term health.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, intimidation, or stalking.
- Sexualized language, imagery, or unwanted attention.
- Trolling, insults, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private information without explicit permission.
- Conduct reasonably considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This policy applies in every project space.

It also applies when someone officially represents Xquik-dev.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to [support@xquik.com](mailto:support@xquik.com).

Maintainers will review reports promptly, fairly, and confidentially.

Maintainers may correct, warn, temporarily ban, or permanently ban participants.

Enforcement will reflect the behavior's context, impact, and recurrence.
